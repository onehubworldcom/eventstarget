Free Things to Do This Weekend: A Simple Guide to Planning on a Budget

Looking for a fun weekend without spending a lot? Here is a practical way to find free activities, local events and community plans in cities across the US and Canada.

Planning note: Event dates, times, prices, availability and admission rules can change. Always confirm details with the official organizer before attending.

Start with your city

Begin with Eventstarget city search and narrow your idea to a location. Searching by city first helps you avoid irrelevant results and makes it easier to compare activities.

Search by activity

Think beyond concerts. Outdoor movies, markets, museum programs, cultural events, community festivals and public performances can all make a great weekend plan.

Check the official event page

Event details can change. Always confirm the date, time, location, admission rules and organizer information on the official event page before attending.

Build a simple weekend plan

Pick one main activity and one flexible backup. This keeps your plan simple if weather, capacity or schedules change.
