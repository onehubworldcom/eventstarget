Festivals

A Guide to Fall Festivals Across the USA

Fall brings food festivals, harvest celebrations, arts events, outdoor markets and community gatherings to cities and towns across the country.

Planning note: Event dates, times, prices, availability and admission rules can change. Always confirm details with the official organizer before attending.

Food and harvest festivals

Look for local food, harvest and seasonal celebrations. Many communities combine vendors, performances and family activities in one location.

Arts and craft events

Arts, craft and maker events are another strong fall category. They can be a good option when you want a slower-paced day.

Outdoor community events

Outdoor events are weather dependent, so check the organizer page shortly before leaving. Bring layers and plan a backup activity when appropriate.

Plan around the weather

Compare event dates, locations, admission details and official ticket pages before making travel plans.
